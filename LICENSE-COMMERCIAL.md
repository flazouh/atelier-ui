# atelier-ui commercial license

atelier-ui is free under the [GPL-3.0](LICENSE). An app that ships atelier-ui and does not release its own
source under GPL-3.0 compatible terms needs a commercial license.

A commercial license lets you use, change and ship atelier-ui in closed-source apps, without the GPL's terms.
The price and terms are set in a written agreement with the copyright holder.

To buy one, open an issue at [github.com/flazouh/atelier-ui](https://github.com/flazouh/atelier-ui/issues)
with the title "Commercial license", or contact [@flazouh](https://github.com/flazouh).
